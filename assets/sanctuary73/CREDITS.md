# Sanctuary environment · v73

New AI-generated fan-game environment artwork,2026-11-05. The original forest
illustration in [stage37](../trickcal-stage37/CREDITS.md) was a style/palette
reference. This is NOT an extracted Trickcal map pack or PokeIsaac artwork.
No character image was generated, recolored, cut up or overwritten.

- `room.png`: complete mossy sanctuary walls and clear stone combat floor.
- `objects.png`: transparent4×2 sprite sheet, open/closed/treasure/boss arches,
  chest, pedestal, merchant display and secret wall. Whole sprites share measured
  foot anchors rather than the varying transparent bottom margins.
- Runtime WebPs are format/quality conversions only. PNG sources and the
  original generation outputs under workspace `generated_images` are preserved.
- [sources.json](sources.json) pins all4 files, dimensions, eight foot anchors
  and minimum33px alpha gutter. Candidate environment art is not an approved
  replacement for Butter's character master.
